<FeatureBand n={1} kicker="Stages" title="Watch it sharpen" href="/nani-pix-bot/players/guessing/" more="Guessing and stages" hue="#d99a2b">
  Every round starts as a handful of chunky pixels. Each stage allows a few wrong guesses; once they're used up, the bot posts a clearer picture. Five stages take it from a blur to the real screenshot.

  <StageStrip slot="visual" caption="Wrong guesses each stage allows, by default" />
</FeatureBand>

<FeatureBand n={2} kicker="Start a round" title="Any anime, any scene" href="/nani-pix-bot/players/starting-a-round/" more="Starting a round" hue="#8b6cf0" flip>
  Send the bot a screenshot in a private chat, or type `/newgame` and pick one from Shikimori, Tenrai or TMDB. Then tell the bot which anime it is: search a site, pick it from your MyAnimeList list, or type the title yourself.

  <TelegramChat slot="visual" label="Example: choosing how to identify the anime">
    <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" buttons={[['AniList', 'Shikimori'], ['Tenrai', 'TMDB'], ['Manual entry'], ['My MAL List']]}>
      How would you like to identify the anime?
    </TgMessage>
  </TelegramChat>
</FeatureBand>

<FeatureBand n={3} kicker="Guess" title="Guess together" href="/nani-pix-bot/players/how-a-round-ends/" more="How a round ends" hue="#26b3a3">
  The whole group guesses with `/guess` in the game topic. Case, punctuation and small typos don't matter, and a near miss shows the words you got right. The first right answer wins, and the winner starts the next round.

  <TelegramChat slot="visual" label="Example: guessing in the game topic">
    <TgMessage out>/guess frieren the mage</TgMessage>
    <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">🔎 Partly right: _ _ _ _ _ _ &nbsp; _ _ &nbsp; Frieren</TgMessage>
    <TgMessage out>/guess sousou no frieren</TgMessage>
    <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">🎉 @kenji got it! It was Sousou no Frieren. Your turn to start the next game!</TgMessage>
  </TelegramChat>
</FeatureBand>

<FeatureBand n={4} kicker="Pixels" title="Earn pixels, buy clues" href="/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/" more="Pixels and the clue shop" hue="#e0609e" flip>
  Wrong guesses, quick starts and wins all pay pixels. Spend them on private clues, like a letter, the title's shape or an extra screenshot, or add them to a round's bounty. A HARD MODE win pays double.

  <TelegramChat slot="visual" label="Example: the clue shop">
    <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" buttons={[['🔤 Last letter — 60 💠'], ['🔤 First letter — 100 💠'], ['📏 Title shape — 120 💠'], ['🖼 Extra screenshot — 150 💠'], ['🧩 Reveal a tile — 100 💠']]}>
      🛒 Clue shop — balance: 340 💠
    </TgMessage>
  </TelegramChat>
</FeatureBand>

<FeatureBand n={5} kicker="Achievements" title="Collect achievements" href="/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/" more="Achievements and titles" hue="#e8c547">
  Winning, hosting and playing day after day all unlock achievements. They pay pixels and points, the best ones give you a title to show next to your name, and a few stay secret until someone earns them. Each unlock is announced with its own card.

  <Image slot="visual" class="band-card" src={unlockGold} alt="An achievement card background: a girl in a kimono watching paper lanterns at night" width={720} />
</FeatureBand>

<FeatureBand n={6} kicker="Leaderboard" title="Climb the board" href="/nani-pix-bot/players/leaderboard-and-history/" more="Leaderboard and history" hue="#4aa3e8" flip>
  The all-time leaderboard ranks everyone by wins. Weekly, monthly and yearly races crown their own champions, and `/history` keeps every finished round with all of its guesses.

  <LeaderboardMock slot="visual" label="Example: the leaderboard" header="👑 Leaderboard" player="Player" />
</FeatureBand>
