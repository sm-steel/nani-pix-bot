## Pausing new rounds

`/setgamesenabled off` stops anyone from starting a new round, which is handy while
you're retuning the settings. A round already in progress carries on.
`/setgamesenabled on` lifts the pause.

## Letting the bot start rounds

`/setautostart on` lets the bot start [HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/)
rounds by itself (it's **off** by default):

- when nobody has started a round for 24 hours after the turn opened up, and
- occasionally, right after a round ends, by taking the next turn itself.

The bot picks a random, reasonably well-known anime for these rounds: only TV
series and films (no specials, OVAs or recaps, which often reuse their parent
show's footage), and never adult content. If, after 24 hours without a round, it can't
find a suitable anime with screenshots, it tries again an hour later.

The bot never starts a round while another one is being set up, played or voted
on. `/setautostart off` turns it off again. While new rounds are paused with
`/setgamesenabled off`, the bot doesn't start any either.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are a daily window, for example overnight, when the bot says nothing
on its own. They're **off** by default.

1. Send `/timezone` and pick your time zone from the list, or type it, for example
   `/timezone Europe/Moscow`. Daylight saving time is followed automatically. The
   time zone is saved for you personally, so each admin can have their own.
2. Send `/quiethours 23:00 08:00` to set the window in your time zone. It can cross
   midnight, but it can't start and end at the same time. The bot repeats it back,
   along with the same window in UTC.

If you haven't set a time zone yet, `/quiethours` asks for one first, with a
button for each of the common zones: Europe/Kaliningrad, Europe/Moscow,
Asia/Yekaterinburg, Europe/Berlin, Europe/London and UTC. Tap yours (or send
`/timezone` with any other zone), then send the command again.

Tapping a zone replaces the question with the confirmation.

`/quiethours` on its own shows the current window, and `/quiethours off` turns it
off.

**During quiet hours** the bot holds back everything it would normally do by
itself: reminders, automatic stage changes, timeouts, turn expiry, the end of a
vote, achievement and champion announcements and rounds of its own. The clocks
behind them are paused, so quiet time doesn't count. Anything a player does still
works immediately, including guessing and winning.

A win during quiet hours always hands the turn to the winner: the bot doesn't
take a chance at claiming it for a round of its own.

Setting or changing quiet hours doesn't recalculate deadlines that are already
running. If one of them falls inside the new window, it simply waits until the
window ends. Every clock started after the change (the next guess, the next turn,
the next round) pauses properly.

The quiet-hours time zone is also the group's time zone for champion weeks, months
and years. Without one, the bot uses UTC. The dates you type in `/season` use your
time zone too.
