`/profile` shows your profile as an image card. It works in the game topic and in a
private chat with Nani Pix (in private, for group members only). Add a username to look at someone else's card:
`/profile @username`.

<TelegramChat label="Example: asking for a profile card">
  <TgMessage out>/profile</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="Nani Pix">
    @alice 🌸 · Demo Star
    Seasons played: 2
    Cosmetics owned: 5
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

The card shows:

- your avatar, your name and the [title](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#titles) you chose;
- up to four of your **achievements**, each in its rarity colour (the ones you picked, or
  your rarest);
- up to six season **stamps**, one per finished season you earned Season XP in, with the
  season's full name under it (the ones you picked, or your newest seasons). A stamp
  has the season's art, a **gold ring** once you finished the season's free pass, and a
  golden version if you claimed the season's Premium stamp reward;
- 🏅 how many seasons you have played and 🎁 how many cosmetics you own.

The caption under the card also shows your name flair, if you equipped one.

Here are [Sakura Season](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/sakura-season/#stamps)'s three stamps on a
card: the plain one, the one with the gold ring and the golden Sakura+.

![A profile card with three Sakura stamps: the plain one, the one with a gold ring and the golden Sakura+](../../../assets/generated/profile-stamps-en.webp)

The card's background is your equipped **profile banner**. Without one, it is the key
visual of the last season you played, or a dark gradient if you haven't played a season
yet. A grid of faint glass pixels sits over the top-left corner and fades out toward the
middle; it turns light or dark to suit the picture under it. The picture itself is never
darkened, and every text and counter on the card has a thin dark outline, so it reads on
any banner.

If the username isn't known to Nani Pix, she answers that she doesn't know that player.

## Browsing other players

The card comes with buttons that work right in the same message:

- **◀ Prev** and **Next ▶** switch to the previous or next player, wrapping around at
  the ends;
- **👥 Players** turns the message into a list of players, eight per page; tap a
  name to open that card, or **↩ My card** to go back to yours.

Both walk the players who actually play: everyone who has hosted, won or guessed in a
finished game, the most games first (then by name). Nani Pix isn't listed, and neither is
someone who has only ever lurked, though you can always open their card with
`/profile @username`. You are always in the list, at the end until your first game.

Only the person who sent `/profile` can use the buttons on that card. Anyone else gets a
short note to send `/profile` themselves.

## Choosing what your card shows

On your own card, in a private chat with Nani Pix, there is one more button:
**✏️ Choose what my card shows**. It opens two tabs, **Season stamps** and
**Achievements**, listing everything you hold; ✅ marks what is on your card.

- Tap one to show or hide it. They appear on the card in the order you tick them.
- The card fits up to **6 stamps** and **4 achievements**. Once you're at the limit, hide
  one before adding another.
- For an achievement with several tiers, only the highest one you hold is listed.
- With nothing picked, the card shows your newest seasons and your rarest achievements
  (rarest first, then the highest tier, then the most recent).

**↩ Done** brings the card back with your changes.
