Every round the **bot starts by itself** is a HARD MODE round (see
[How a round ends](/nani-pix-bot/players/how-a-round-ends/#when-the-bot-starts-a-round-itself)).
Rounds that players start are never HARD MODE.

## The rules

- **Two turns instead of five stages.**
- **Two screenshots at once.** Each turn shows two different screenshots of the
  same anime, posted together as an album.
- **Turn 1 isn't blurrier than usual.** It's as blocky as stage 1 of a normal
  round with the default settings; turn 2 is noticeably clearer. What makes HARD
  MODE hard is the guess limit.
- **One wrong guess per turn.** One miss on turn 1 moves to turn 2, where both
  screenshots are clearer. One miss on turn 2 ends the round.
- **Double reward.** Cracking it counts as **+2** wins on the leaderboard, and the
  pixel reward is doubled too.
- **Clues are on sale.** Every clue in the shop is discounted, starting at 20%.
  The discount grows by 20 points after each HARD MODE round nobody cracks, up to
  80%, and drops back to 20% once one is solved. The round's first post states the
  discount.
- **The shop is a link.** An album can't carry buttons, so instead of the
  🛒 Clues button, a HARD MODE post ends with a link that opens the shop in a
  private chat with the bot.
- **No `/correct` and no `/sharpen`.** Nobody hosts the round, so the vote below
  replaces `/correct`.

Everything else is the same as a normal round: how guesses are matched, the
two-day limit, and the winner getting the next turn.

## The vote

The bot's matching is strict, and with one guess per turn a correct answer under
an unusual name can slip through. So when a HARD MODE round ends without a correct
guess, but somebody did guess, **the group votes** on whether one of the guessers
was actually right.

- The bot posts the answer, then a ballot with **one button per guesser**, showing
  what each of them guessed and how many votes they have so far. The counts update
  as people vote.
- Any group member can vote, once, and can change their vote by tapping another
  button. You can't vote for yourself.
- The vote closes after **15 minutes**, and the buttons disappear. If the group
  has [quiet hours](/nani-pix-bot/players/how-a-round-ends/#quiet-hours), they
  push the close back like any other timer.
- A guesser wins if they get **the most votes, at least 3, with no tie**. They get
  a normal HARD MODE win, **+30 💠** for the wait (by default), and the next turn.
- Otherwise the round stays unsolved.

While a vote is open, no new round can start, and `/guess` only points you to the
ballot. An admin
can also settle it directly (see [Fixing a round](/nani-pix-bot/admins/fixing-things/)),
and an admin's [`/stop`](/nani-pix-bot/players/how-a-round-ends/#stopping-a-round)
cancels it and refunds every clue and the bounty.

## Clue cashback

When a HARD MODE round finally ends unsolved, everyone who bought clues for it gets
**half of what they spent** back (admins can change the share). That's true however
it ends unsolved: after a vote that picks nobody, or with no vote at all because
nobody guessed. Any bounty is refunded in full, as in a normal round. You keep the
cashback even if an admin later names you the winner.

A winner chosen by the vote or by an admin also gets **+30 💠** (by default) on
top of the normal HARD MODE win, for having had to wait for it.
