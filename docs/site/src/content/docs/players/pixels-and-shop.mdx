**Pixels 💠** are the game's currency. You start with 50, earn more by playing, and
spend them on private clues and a few public perks. Check your balance any time
with `/balance`.

The amounts below are the defaults. Your group's admins can change any of them.

## Earning pixels

| What happened | Pixels | Who gets them |
|---|---|---|
| A wrong guess | 2 each, up to 10 per round | the guesser |
| The first guess of a round, right or wrong | 5 | whoever guessed first |
| Winning at stage 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 | 40 / 30 / 25 / 20 / 15 | the winner |
| Your round was solved at stage 2, 3 or 4 | 15 | the round's creator |
| You started your round within an hour of getting the turn | 10 | the round's creator |
| The round's bounty | the whole pot | the winner |

A HARD MODE win pays double. Achievements pay pixels too (see
[Achievements and titles](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/)). The bot's replies
show what each guess or win just earned.

## The clue shop

Clues are **private hints for the round in play**, delivered to you in a private
message. Open the shop with the 🛒 button under the round's picture, or send
`/shop` to the bot. Anyone in the group can buy, except the round's creator.

| Clue | Default price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 🔤 Last letter | 60 | the last letter of the title |
| 🔤 First letter | 100 | the first letter of the title |
| 📏 Title shape | 120 | the title with every letter hidden as `_`, so you see how many words and letters it has |
| 🖼 Extra screenshot | 150, then 225, then 300 | another screenshot from the same anime, pixelated to the current stage (up to 3 per round) |
| 🧩 Reveal a tile | 100 | one square of the picture shown sharp |

A few details:

- **Letter clues and the title shape** cover several versions of the title (for
  example English and romaji), each one labelled. Numbers and the word "season"
  are skipped, because guessing ignores them anyway.
- **Reveal a tile**: the first buyer in a round picks which of 64 squares to
  reveal. Everyone who buys it later in that round gets the same square. It isn't
  sold in HARD MODE.
- **The group sees that you bought a clue**, but not what it says. Every clue has
  a **📣 Share with the group** button if you want to post it for everyone.
- **You never pay for nothing.** If a clue can't be delivered, or no unused
  screenshot is left, you're refunded or never charged. If the round is stopped,
  every clue bought for it is refunded.

## Spending in public

- **`/bounty <amount>`** adds pixels to the round's pot (at least 30). Anyone can
  add, any number of times, including the round's creator. The winner takes the
  whole pot. If nobody wins, or the round is stopped, everyone gets their
  contribution back. The pot is shown under every stage's picture.
- **`/sharpen`** pays 250 to move the round to the next, clearer stage straight
  away. The bot asks you to confirm first. Not available on stage 5, in HARD MODE,
  or to the round's creator.
- **`/tip @username <amount>`** gives some of your pixels to another player, in the
  game topic or in a private message.
