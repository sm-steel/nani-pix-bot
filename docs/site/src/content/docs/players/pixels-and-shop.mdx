**Pixels 💠** are the game's currency. You start with 50, earn more by playing, and
spend them on private clues and a few public perks. Check your balance any time
with `/balance`, in the game topic or in a private chat with the bot.

<TelegramChat label="Example: checking your balance">
  <TgMessage out>/balance</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">Your balance: 140 💠</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

The amounts below are the defaults. Your group's admins can change any of them.

## Earning pixels

| What happened | Pixels | Who gets them |
|---|---|---|
| A wrong guess | 2 each, up to 10 per round | the guesser |
| The first guess of a round, right or wrong | 5 | whoever guessed first |
| Winning at stage 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 | 40 / 30 / 25 / 20 / 15 | the winner |
| Your round was solved at stage 2, 3 or 4 | 15 | the round's creator |
| You started your round within an hour of being handed the turn | 10 | the round's creator |
| The round's bounty | the whole pot | the winner |
| You were named the winner by the group vote or by an admin | 30, on top of the normal win | that winner |
| A HARD MODE round you bought clues for ends unsolved | half of what you spent on its clues | every clue buyer |

A few details:

- **The quick-start bonus** only counts when the turn was handed to you, by
  winning the last round or by someone passing it with `/skip @you`. Starting a
  round while the turn is open to everyone earns nothing extra.
- **A HARD MODE win pays double**, but nobody gets the creator's bonuses for it:
  the bot started that round itself.
- **The HARD MODE cashback** is half of what you actually paid for that round's
  clues (after the discount, minus anything refunded). It's paid once, when the
  round finally ends unsolved, after the vote if there is one.

Achievements pay pixels too (see
[Achievements and titles](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/)). The bot's replies
show what each guess or win just earned.

## The clue shop

Clues are **private hints for the round in play**, delivered to you in a private
message. Anyone in the group can buy while a round is being played, except the
round's creator.

Open the shop with the **🛒 Clues** button under the round's picture (in a HARD
MODE round, the 🛒 link in the caption), or send `/shop` to the bot. The menu shows
your balance and only what you can buy right now:

- an item you can't afford yet has a 🔒 on it;
- a clue you already have, or one that isn't sold in this round, isn't listed;
- when nothing is left for you, the bot says *Nothing left to buy in this round.*

| Clue | Default price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 🔤 Last letter | 60 | the last letter of the title |
| 🔤 First letter | 100 | the first letter of the title |
| 📏 Title shape | 120 | the title with every letter hidden as `_`, so you see how many words and letters it has |
| 🖼 Extra screenshot | 150, then 225, then 300 | another screenshot from the same anime, pixelated to the current stage (up to 3 per player per round) |
| 🧩 Reveal a tile | 100 | one square of the picture shown sharp |

A few details:

- **Letter clues and the title shape** cover several versions of the title (for
  example English and romaji), each one labelled. Numbers and the word "season"
  are skipped, because guessing ignores them anyway.
- **The title shape fills in letters you bought.** If you already have the first
  or last letter, it shows them in place. Buy a letter after the shape and the bot
  sends you the updated shape as well.
- **The title shape leaves the shop** once a guess in the round has been
  [partly right](/nani-pix-bot/players/guessing/#partly-right), since the shape is
  then public. If you bought it before, you keep it.
- **Extra screenshots** come only from Shikimori or TMDB, so the clue is offered
  only when the round's anime is on one of them. Tenrai is never used: its
  pictures are posters, which can show the title. You never get a picture that's
  already in play in this round or one you've already bought. The bot finds the
  screenshot before it charges you, so if none is left you pay nothing.
- **Reveal a tile** isn't sold in HARD MODE (see below for how it works).
- **Buttons from an earlier round** do nothing and spend nothing; the bot tells
  you that round is over.

### Revealing a tile

The first player to buy **🧩 Reveal a tile** in a round chooses which square to
reveal, on an 8×8 grid laid over the picture. Opening the grid is free: you're
charged only when you tap a square. Everyone who buys the clue later in that round
gets the same square straight away, and is charged when they tap the shop button.
Each player can buy it once per round.

<TelegramChat label="Example: picking the round's tile">
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" buttons={tileGrid}>
    🧩 Pick the round's tile to reveal (everyone gets the same one):
  </TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" image={stage3} buttons={[['📣 Share with the group']]}>
    🧩 Your unpixelated tile.
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

The picture shows the round's current stage, with your square shown sharp.

### What the group sees

**The group sees that you bought a clue**, but not what it says. Every clue has a
**📣 Share with the group** button if you want to post it for everyone. Sharing is
free, works once per clue, and only while the round is still being played. The
one exception is a title shape that has gained a letter since you shared it: you
can share that one again. After a share, Telegram shows a short *Shared with the
group.* note and the button disappears.

### You never pay for nothing

- If a clue can't be delivered to you, you're refunded straight away.
- If no unused screenshot is left, or one can't be loaded, you're never charged.
- If the round is stopped, every clue bought for it is refunded.
- An admin can refund a single clue purchase (see
  [Fixing a round](/nani-pix-bot/admins/fixing-things/)). The bot sends you a
  private message when that happens.

## Spending in public

### Bounty

**`/bounty <amount>`** adds pixels to the round's pot (at least 30). It works only
in the game topic, while a round is being played. Anyone can add, any number of
times, including the round's creator.

<TelegramChat label="Example: adding to the bounty">
  <TgMessage out>/bounty 50</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">💰 Yuki adds 50 💠 to the bounty — the pot is now 80 💠.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

The pot is shown under every stage's picture and in `/status`. The winner takes
the whole pot, shown as its own line in the win message (*💰 Kenji takes the
bounty: +80 💠*). If nobody wins, or the round is stopped, everyone gets their
contribution back.

### Sharpen

**`/sharpen`** pays 250 to move the round to the next, clearer stage straight
away. Not available on stage 5, in HARD MODE, or to the round's creator.

The bot asks you to confirm first. Only you can press the buttons under your
request. If the stage changes before you confirm, the button is refused and
nothing is charged: send `/sharpen` again. Once you confirm, your request turns
into *🔍 Sharpened.* and the next stage is posted for everyone, saying who paid
for it.

<TelegramChat label="Example: sharpening the picture">
  <TgMessage out>/sharpen</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" buttons={[['🔍 Sharpen for 250 💠', 'Cancel']]}>
    🔍 Sharpen the image to the next stage for 250 💠? Everyone sees the clearer picture.
  </TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" image={stage3} buttons={[['🛒 Clues']]}>
    🔍 Mio paid 250 💠 to sharpen the image!

    📸 Stage 3/5 (guesses left: {third.wrongGuessLimit}/{third.wrongGuessLimit}) — then it clears further.

    💰 Bounty: 80 💠
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

### Tips

**`/tip @username <amount>`** gives some of your pixels to another player, in the
game topic or in a private message. The smallest tip is 1. You can't tip yourself
or the bot, and the bot has to know the player already: if it doesn't, they need
to send it a private message first. A tip is a plain gift, so it's never
refunded.

<TelegramChat label="Example: tipping a player">
  <TgMessage out>/tip @kenji 20</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">💸 Hana tipped @kenji 20 💠.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>
