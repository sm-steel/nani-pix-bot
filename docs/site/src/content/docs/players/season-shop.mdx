A season may open a **Season Shop**: a stall where you spend the Season Coins 🪙 you earned
in the season's games. Seasons themselves arrive in a later update, so nothing here is live
yet; this page describes how it will work.

Send `/seasonshop` to the bot in a private chat (group members only). It shows your 🪙
balance and everything on offer, with a mark on what you can't buy any more.

## Buying

1. **Tap an item** to see it first: a cosmetic is previewed the way it is in
   [`/cosmetics`](/nani-pix-bot/players/cosmetics/), and a clue token 🎟 shows a
   golden-ticket icon.
2. Tap **Buy for N 🪙** to buy it, or **« Back** to return to the list.

Everything is paid in 🪙, never in 💠.

- **Cosmetics can be bought once each.** There are no refunds, so look at the preview
  before you tap Buy.
- **Clue tokens 🎟** can be bought up to a limit per season. The list shows how many you
  have left, and *sold out* once you've bought all you can.
- The bot refuses if you can't afford the item, already own it or have reached the limit,
  and in those cases nothing is charged.

A bought cosmetic is added to your collection. **Put it on in
[`/cosmetics`](/nani-pix-bot/players/cosmetics/).** A bought clue token works like one from
the pass: see [Season Coins and the Season Pass](/nani-pix-bot/players/season-pass/).

## Only while the season runs

The shop is **open only while the season is running**. Once the season is closing or over,
the shop is closed, and your leftover 🪙 turn into 💠 (see
[When the season ends](/nani-pix-bot/players/season-pass/#when-the-season-ends)). Spend your
coins before then.

Buying here also counts towards [season achievements](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#season-achievements), such as Window Shopper and Collector.
