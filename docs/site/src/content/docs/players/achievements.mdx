Playing earns **achievements**. Each one pays pixels 💠 and achievement points 🏆,
and the best of them unlock a **title** shown next to your name. On top of that,
every week, month and year crowns its **champions**.

![An example achievement card: @hana unlocked Sharpshooter V, Gold, +200 💠 and +8 🏆](../../../assets/generated/unlock-en.png)

When you unlock something, the bot posts a card like this one, with your avatar on
it, in the game topic. When one moment unlocks three or more at once, the cards go
out together as one album. During [quiet hours](/nani-pix-bot/players/how-a-round-ends/#quiet-hours)
the cards wait and are posted once quiet hours end.

## Rarities and rewards

| Rarity | Pixels (default) | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 🥉 Bronze | 25 | 1 |
| 🥈 Silver | 75 | 3 |
| 🥇 Gold | 200 | 8 |
| 💎 Platinum | 500 | 20 |

Most achievements are **ladders**: you climb them tier by tier, and each tier pays
its own reward. Others are **one-off feats**. A few can only ever be held by one
person in the group. And some achievements are **secret**: they stay hidden until
you earn them, so you'll have to find those yourself.

Only what happens after achievements were added to your group counts; older games
don't.

## Every achievement

These are all the achievements except the secret ones. The medals show each
tier's rarity.

### One-off feats

| Achievement | How to get it | Rarity |
|---|---|---|
| **Kingmaker** | Vote for the player the group picks in a HARD MODE vote | 🥉 |
| **Comeback** | Win after 3 or more wrong guesses of your own in that round | 🥉 |
| **First Try** | The round's very first `/guess` is yours, and it's right | 🥈 |
| **Crowd Pleaser** | Host a round that 5 or more players guess in | 🥈 |
| **People's Champion** | Win a round by the group's vote | 🥈 |
| **Shopaholic** | Buy every kind of clue in one round | 🥈 |
| **Explorer** | Host rounds found in each of the 5 ways: AniList, Shikimori, Tenrai, TMDB and typed in by hand (My MAL List counts as Tenrai) | 🥈 |
| **Clutch** | Win at the last stage with the last guess it allowed | 🥇 |
| **Speed Demon** | Win within 60 seconds of the round going live | 🥇 |

First Try, Comeback, Clutch and Speed Demon only count in normal rounds, not HARD
MODE.

### Only one per group

Only one person can ever hold each of these. Once someone has Pioneer, everyone
else sees it locked, marked **🔒 taken by @them**. Milestone Keeper has one holder
per milestone, and the list always shows the next one still to be won.

| Achievement | How to get it | Rarity |
|---|---|---|
| **Pioneer** | The group's first win after achievements were added | 💎 |
| **Milestone Keeper** | Win the group's 100th, 250th, 500th or 1000th solved round, then every 500th after that. Each milestone has its own holder | 🥇100 · 🥇250 · 💎500 · 💎1000 and on |

Milestone Keeper counts **solved** rounds since achievements were added, not the 🎲
game number: that number also counts unsolved and cancelled rounds and older
games. The full list shows how far the group has got, e.g. *solved so far:
137/250*.

### Champion achievements

**Champion of the Week** 🥈, **of the Month** 🥇 and **of the Year** 💎 go to
whoever tops that period's race (see [Champions](#champions) below). You can win
them again every period.

## Refunds and spending

- **A refund never takes progress away.** Whatever you'd already earned stays
  earned, and the money coming back doesn't count as income for Pixel Magnate.
- **A refunded clue still counts** for Clue Collector and Shopaholic, and the 💠
  you spent on it still counts for Big Spender.
- **A bounty counts for Big Spender only when it pays out** to a winner. A bounty
  that came back to you because nobody won never counted.

## Seeing your achievements

- **`/achievements`** in the game topic shows a summary: how many you have, your
  points, your place, your title and your latest unlocks. Its **📖 Browse in DM**
  button opens the full list in a private message.
- **`/achievements @username`** shows someone else's, and **`/achievements top`**
  the top 10 by points.

The full list in a private message is one message that changes in place as you
tap its buttons:

  to turn pages.
- A ladder takes one line: the tier you're on and, after it, *next: 12/25*, your

## Titles

The top tier of every ladder, each one-per-group achievement and every champion
win unlock a title. Choose which one appears next to your name with `/title` in a
private message, or choose **No title**. Titles show on the leaderboard, in the
achievements top and in your `/achievements` summary.

## Champions

![An example champions card: the top three players of 2025 on a podium](../../../assets/generated/podium-en.png)

Every week, month and year is a race for 🌟 points, earned from wins in that period:

| How you won | 🌟 |
|---|---|
| At stage 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 | 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 |
| HARD MODE, turn 1 / turn 2 | 6 / 4 |
| A clean solve: a normal round won with no wrong guess of your own in it | +1 |
| Hosting a round someone solved | +1 |

An unsolved round gives nobody 🌟.

- **Periods** follow the group's time zone: weeks start on Monday at 00:00,
  months on the 1st and years on 1 January.
- **A round counts in the period it ended in.** If an admin names a winner later
  with `/setwinner`, the win counts on the day the round originally ended.
- **Ties** go to more wins in the period, then fewer wrong guesses in the rounds
  you won, then less total time to solve. Players still equal share the place.
- **When a period ends**, the bot posts the podium with everyone placed 1st to 3rd
  (a tie can put more than three people there). Whoever is in first place becomes
  champion and gets the Champion achievement and its title. If two or three people
  share first place, they all become champion; if **four or more** share it,
  nobody is crowned that period, though the podium is still posted. A period in
  which nobody scored isn't posted at all. Like achievement cards, these posts wait
  out quiet hours.

### The standings

**`/standings`** works in the game topic and in a private message. It shows the
running week, month and year in one message: the top 5 of each, with 🌟 points, 👑
wins in the period, ❌ wrong guesses and ⏱ total time to solve (the last two are
the tie-breakers). Below each table is your own place if you're not in the top 5,
or a note that you haven't scored yet. The "ends" time is in the group's time zone
too (UTC if admins haven't set one for quiet hours).

Its two buttons open a private message with the bot:

- **📖 How points work** explains the scoring.
- **🕑 Recent changes** lists this week's 🌟 gains, newest first, 10 per page:
  when, who, how many points, why (which round they won and at which stage, or
  which round they hosted; ✨ marks a clean solve) and how their place in the week
  changed.

### In win messages

Every win message also shows the 🌟 it earned and where that puts the winner in
each race, plus the host's point:

<TelegramChat label="Example: the 🌟 part of a win message">
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">
    🌟 @hana: +4 points

    • this week — 14 🌟, 1st place (⬆1)

    • this month — 31 🌟, 2nd place

    • this year — 120 🌟, shared 1st place

    🌟 Host @kenji: +1 point
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

*(⬆1)* means places gained, and *(new on the board)* marks a first score in that
period.
