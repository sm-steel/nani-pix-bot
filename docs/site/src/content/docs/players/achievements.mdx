Playing earns **achievements**. Each one pays pixels 💠 and achievement points 🏆,
and the best of them unlock a **title** shown next to your name. On top of that,
every week, month and year crowns its **champions**.

![An achievement card background: an anime girl cheering at a golden sunset](../../../assets/cards/unlock-gold.png)

When you unlock something, the bot posts a card like this one, with your avatar on
it, in the game topic.

## Rarities and rewards

| Rarity | Pixels (default) | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 25 | 1 |
| Silver | 75 | 3 |
| Gold | 200 | 8 |
| Platinum | 500 | 20 |

Most achievements are **ladders**: you climb them tier by tier, and each tier pays
its own reward. A few examples:

- **Sharpshooter**: rounds you've won.
- **Storyteller**: rounds you hosted that someone solved.
- **Persistent**: rounds you guessed in at all.
- **Regular** and **Unbroken**: days you played, and days in a row.
- **Eagle Eye**: wins at the very first stage.
- **Stumper**: rounds you hosted that stumped at least three guessers.
- **Hard Mode Hero**: HARD MODE wins.

Others are **one-off feats**, like **First Try** (the round's very first guess is
yours, and it's right) or **Speed Demon** (winning within a minute of the round
starting). A couple can only ever be held by one person in the group, such as
**Pioneer**. And some achievements are **secret**: they stay hidden until
someone earns them, so you'll have to find those yourself.

Only what happens after achievements were added to your group counts; older games
don't.

## Seeing your achievements

- **`/achievements`** in the game topic shows a summary: how many you have, your
  points, rank, title and latest unlocks. Its **📖 Browse in DM** button opens the
  full list.
- **`/achievements @username`** shows someone else's, and **`/achievements top`**
  the top 10 by points.
- The full list, in a private message, can show everything, only what you've
  earned, or only what you haven't, with your progress towards the next tier. You
  can also **compare** your achievements with another player's.

## Titles

The top tier of every ladder, every one-person achievement and every champion win
unlock a title. Choose which one appears next to your name with `/title` in a
private message, or choose none. Titles show on the leaderboard and in the bot's
announcements.

## Champions

![A podium card background](../../../assets/cards/podium-year.png)

Every week, month and year is a race for 🌟 points, earned from wins in that period:

| How you won | 🌟 |
|---|---|
| At stage 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 | 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 |
| HARD MODE, turn 1 / turn 2 | 6 / 4 |
| A clean solve: a normal round won with no wrong guess of your own in it | +1 |
| Hosting a round someone solved | +1 |

- **Ties** go to more wins in the period, then fewer wrong guesses in the rounds
  you won, then less total time to solve. Players still equal share the place.
- **`/standings`** shows the running week, month and year: the top 5 of each, plus
  your own place. Its buttons explain the scoring and list this week's changes.
- **When a period ends** (at midnight in the group's time zone), the bot posts the
  podium. Everyone in first place becomes champion and gets the Champion
  achievement and title.

Every win message also shows the 🌟 it earned and where that puts the winner in
each race.
