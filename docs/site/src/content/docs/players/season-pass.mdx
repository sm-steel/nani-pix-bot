While a season is running, your games earn two things besides pixels: **Season XP**, which
climbs the pass, and **Season Coins 🪙**. Seasons themselves arrive in a later update, so
nothing here is live yet; this page describes how it will work.

## Season Coins 🪙

Season Coins are a **separate balance** from your pixels 💠, kept per season. You earn them
only in games that count for the season:

| What happened | Coins |
|---|---|
| You won (the same at any stage) | the season's win amount |
| Your round was solved or ended unsolved | the season's hosting amount |
| Your first guess in a game | the season's first-guess amount |
| A pass reward that pays coins | the amount on the tier |

Each season sets its own amounts. Bot-started HARD MODE rounds pay no hosting coins, just
like Season XP. If an admin names a winner for your round after it ended unsolved, you get
the difference on top, so you end up with exactly the solved amount, in coins and Season XP
alike.

## The Season Pass

The pass is a ladder of **tiers**, reached with Season XP. Every tier has a **free reward**
and, if the season has Premium, a **Premium reward** on top. A reward is a cosmetic item,
🪙 coins, 💠 pixels or a clue token 🎟.

- Rewards are **granted automatically**, in order and once each, as your XP reaches a tier.
  You can keep earning them while a finished season is still closing on its last games, but
  never after it has ended.
- Each grant sends you **a private message** listing what you got. An item you already owned
  is marked "already yours".
- When you finish the free track, the game topic gets a single line about it.

### /seasonpass

Send `/seasonpass` to the bot in a private chat (group members only). It shows your Season
XP, your tier (n of N), the XP left for the next one, your 🪙 balance, whether you have
Premium, and every tier: ✅ or ▫️ for the free reward, ✅ or 🔒 ⭐ for the Premium one.

## Premium ⭐

Premium unlocks the second reward of every tier. Buy it from `/seasonpass` with the
**⭐ Buy Premium** button, then confirm; it costs 💠 pixels, at the price the season sets.

- You can buy it **only while the season is running**, not once it is closing or over.
- It is **retroactive**: every Premium reward for tiers you have already reached arrives at
  once, and the rest as you reach them.
- The bot refuses if you already have it, can't afford it, or the season has no Premium.

## Clue tokens 🎟

Some tiers give a clue token. While you hold one, **your next clue in the
[clue shop](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/#the-clue-shop) is free**: it uses the
token instead of 💠, whichever clue it is. The shop shows 🎟 where the price would be. If the
clue can't be delivered to you, the token comes back. A clue bought with a token shows 🎟 in
the game history. A pass hands out at most two tokens.

## Objectives 🎯

A season may set **objectives**: one-time goals that pay Season XP and Season Coins 🪙 when
you complete them, such as hosting a few games, winning by a certain stage, guessing in
several games, sharing clues, sending tips or playing on different days. Each objective is
done once per season and pays once.

- **Only the season's games count.** Hosting, wins, guesses and clue shares in games that
  belong to the season move your progress; games outside it don't. **Tips** count when you
  give them while the season is running.
- A win "by stage N" means you won at stage N or earlier. HARD MODE wins don't count toward
  it.
- When you complete one, the bot sends you **a private message** with its name and reward.
- `/seasonpass` lists every objective with your progress (for example 2/5) and its reward.
  A finished one shows the date you completed it (DD.MM).

## When the season ends

You can spend 🪙 before then in the [Season Shop](/nani-pix-bot/players/season-shop/).
Every leftover 🪙 balance **turns into 💠** at the season's rate (normally 2 🪙 for 1 💠).
An odd coin that can't make a full 💠 is dropped, and every balance ends up at zero. The
season's end post says how many players' coins became how much 💠, and at what rate.

Seasons also have [achievements](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#season-achievements) of their own.
