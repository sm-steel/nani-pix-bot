## Someone wins

The first correct `/guess` wins the round. The bot posts a short video in which the
pixelated picture turns into the original screenshot, names the anime, and
congratulates the winner, whose avatar appears in a gold badge.

**The winner starts the next round.** After 15 minutes the bot reminds them in a
private message (or with a mention in the topic, if it can't message them). If they
haven't started within 12 hours, the turn opens to everyone.

**When the bot misses a right answer.** If someone gave a correct answer under a
name the bot didn't recognise, the round's creator can award them the win with
`/correct @username` in the game topic. It only works while the round is running,
and the creator can't award it to themselves.

## Nobody wins

A round ends **unsolved** when:

- the last try on stage 5 is a wrong guess,
- nobody guesses on stage 5 for 6 hours, or
- **two days** pass since the round went live, however many guesses were made.

The bot reveals the screenshot and the title. An unsolved round doesn't hand the
turn to anyone: if the turn was open, it stays open.

## Stopping a round

The round's creator, or any group admin, can stop a round by sending `/stop` to the
bot in a **private message**. The bot always asks first:

- **🛑 Yes, stop it** ends the round quietly.
- **🔎 Stop and reveal** ends it and shows the group what the anime was.

A stopped round doesn't count as a win or a loss. Any clues bought and any bounty
are refunded in full, and the turn opens to everyone. `/stop` also cancels a round
you're still setting up.

## Passing your turn

If it's your turn and you don't want to start a round, use `/skip` in the game
topic:

- `/skip` opens the turn to everyone.
- `/skip @username` hands it to someone specific. They get the same reminder and
  12-hour window.

`/skip` only works while no round is running, and only for the player whose turn it
is.

## When the bot starts a round itself

If the group's admins have turned it on, the bot can start rounds by itself:

- **When nobody starts one for 24 hours** after the turn opened up.
- **Occasionally right after a round ends.** There's a small chance (about 1 in 8)
  that the bot "steals" the next turn. If you've just won, you still keep the win
  and everything it earned; you just don't get to host the next round.

Rounds the bot starts are always [HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/).

## Quiet hours

Your group may have **quiet hours** set, for example overnight. During them the bot
sends nothing on its own: no reminders, no automatic stage changes, no timeouts, no
rounds of its own. Its timers are paused too, so quiet time doesn't count towards
them. Everything you do still works normally, including guessing and winning.
