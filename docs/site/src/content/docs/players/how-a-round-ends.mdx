## Someone wins

The first correct `/guess` wins the round. The bot posts a short video in which the
pixelated picture turns into the original screenshot, names the anime, and
congratulates the winner, whose avatar appears in a gold badge. The same post lists
what the win paid: the winner's 💠, the round creator's bonus for a well-pitched
screenshot (for a win on stages 2–4), the [bounty](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/#spending-in-public)
if anyone put one up, and the [champion points](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#champions)
the win added for the winner and the host.

  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" image={original}>
    🎉 Kenji got it! It was Whisper of the Heart. Your turn to start the next game!

    🎲 Game #142

    💠 +25 for Kenji

    🎨 @hana gets +15 💠 for a well-pitched screenshot

    💰 Kenji takes the bounty: +40 💠

    🌟 @kenji: +3 points

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• this week — 7 🌟, 1st place (⬆2)

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• this month — 18 🌟, 2nd place

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• this year — 64 🌟, shared 3rd place

    🌟 Host @hana: +1 point
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

**The winner starts the next round.** After 15 minutes the bot reminds them in a
private message. If it can't message them (they've never started a chat with the
bot), it mentions them in the game topic instead. If it can't even name them
(they have no @username), it posts this, so anyone can open the turn:

<TelegramChat label="Example: the reminder when the bot can't reach or name the player">
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">⏰ It's someone's turn to start a new game, but I can't reach them privately — /skip to open it up to anyone!</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

<TelegramChat label="Example: the turn reminder in a private chat">
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">⏰ Reminder: it's your turn to start a new game! DM me a screenshot whenever you're ready, or send /newgame to pick one instead.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

If they haven't started within 12 hours, the turn opens to everyone and the bot says
so in the topic: "⌛ Nobody started a new game in time — it's open to anyone now!"

**When the bot misses a right answer.** If someone gave a correct answer under a
name the bot didn't recognise, the round's creator can award them the win with
`/correct @username` in the game topic. It works only while the round is running,
and only once somebody has guessed: it's for a guess the bot missed, not a way to
skip to the end. The creator can't award the win to themselves or to the bot, and
the bot has to know the player: anyone who has ever written to the bot or chatted
in the topic is fine, otherwise they need to message the bot first.

<TelegramChat label="Example: the round's creator awarding a missed answer">
  <TgMessage out>/correct @kenji</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" image={original}>
    🎉 Correct! @kenji got it — it was Whisper of the Heart. Your turn to start the next game!

    🎲 Game #142

    🌟 @kenji: +1 point

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• this year — 62 🌟, 3rd place

    🌟 Host @hana: +1 point
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

## Nobody wins

A round ends **unsolved** when:

- the last try on stage 5 is a wrong guess,
- nobody guesses on stage 5 for 6 hours, or
- **two days** pass since the round went live, however many guesses were made.

If nobody guesses for 3 hours, the bot first nudges the topic. After 6 hours without
a guess it posts the next, clearer stage by itself, so a round nobody touches still
ends within 30 hours.

The bot reveals the screenshot and the title with the same kind of video as a win,
just without the gold badge. If the video can't be made in time, you get the plain
screenshot instead. Any [bounty](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/#spending-in-public)
goes back to the players who put it up, and the reveal says so.

An unsolved round doesn't hand the turn to anyone: if the turn was open, it stays
open.

## Stopping a round

The round's creator, or any group admin, can stop a round by sending `/stop` to the
bot in a **private message**. A round the bot started has no human creator, so only
an admin can stop it. The bot always asks first:

- **🛑 Yes, stop it** ends the round quietly.
- **🔎 Stop and reveal** ends it and shows the group what the anime was, with the
  same reveal video as an unsolved round.
- **❌ No, keep going** leaves it running.

"Stop and reveal" is offered only for a round that's already live in the topic. A
round still being set up hasn't shown anything to the group yet, so `/stop` just
asks yes or no and cancels it.

<TelegramChat label="Example: stopping a round from a private chat">
  <TgMessage out>/stop</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" buttons={[['🛑 Yes, stop it'], ['🔎 Stop and reveal'], ['❌ No, keep going']]}>Stop the current game (Whisper of the Heart)? This can't be undone — the turn will open up for anyone to start a new game.</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">Game stopped — the answer was posted to the group.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

<TelegramChat label="Example: what the game topic sees after Stop and reveal">
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" image={original}>
    🛑 The game was stopped. It was Whisper of the Heart.

    Anyone can start a new one now!

    🎲 Game #142
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

A stopped round doesn't count as a win or a loss. Any clues bought and any bounty
are refunded in full, and the turn opens to everyone. This includes a
[HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/#the-vote) round whose vote is still
open: stopping it cancels the vote.

## Passing your turn

If it's your turn and you don't want to start a round, use `/skip` in the game
topic:

- `/skip` opens the turn to everyone.
- `/skip @username` hands it to someone specific. They get the same reminder and
  12-hour window. As with `/correct`, the bot has to know them, and you can't hand
  the turn to the bot.

`/skip` only works while no round is running, and only for the player whose turn it
is. When the turn is already open to everyone, there's nothing to skip, and the bot
says so.

<TelegramChat label="Example: passing the turn">
  <TgMessage out>/skip @yuki</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">Turn passed to @yuki.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

<TelegramChat label="Example: opening the turn to everyone">
  <TgMessage out>/skip</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">Turn opened up — anyone can start the next game!</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

## When the bot starts a round itself

If the group's admins have turned it on, the bot can start rounds by itself:

- **When nobody starts one for 24 hours** after the turn opened up.
- **Occasionally right after a round ends.** There's a small chance (about 1 in 8)
  that the bot "steals" the next turn. If you've just won, you still keep the win
  and everything it earned; you just don't get to host the next round. Losing your
  turn this way does come with a consolation: a hidden
  [achievement](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/).

Rounds the bot starts are always [HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/).

<TelegramChat label="Example: the bot stealing the winner's turn (the first of its two screenshots)">
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" image={stage1}>
    😈 Nice win, Kenji! But I'm stealing your turn for a HARD MODE round — two screenshots, no mercy.

    📸 Turn 1/2 — guesses: 1/1

    🏷 HARD MODE clue sale: −20% on every clue this round.

    🎲 Game #143

    🛒 https://t.me/your_bot?start=shop
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

## Quiet hours

Your group may have **quiet hours** set, for example overnight. During them the bot
sends nothing on its own: no reminders, no automatic stage changes, no timeouts, no
rounds of its own. Its timers are paused too, so quiet time doesn't count towards
them. Everything you do still works normally, including guessing and winning.
