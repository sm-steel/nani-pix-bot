## Someone wins

The first correct `/guess` wins the round. Nani Pix posts a short video in which the
pixelated picture turns into the original screenshot, names the anime, and
congratulates the winner, whose avatar appears in a gold badge. The same post lists
what the win paid: the winner's 💠, the round creator's bonus for a well-pitched
screenshot (for a win on stages 2–4), the [bounty](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/#spending-in-public)
if anyone put one up, and the [champion points](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#champions)
the win added for the winner and the host.

    🌟 @kenji: +3 points

    🌟 Host @hana: +1 point

**The winner starts the next round.** After 15 minutes Nani Pix reminds them in a
private message. If she can't message them (they've never started a chat with
her), she mentions them in the game topic instead. If she can't even name them
(they have no @username), she posts this, so anyone can open the turn:

<TelegramChat label="Example: the reminder when Nani Pix can't reach or name the player">
  <TgMessage from="Nani Pix">⏰ It's someone's turn to start a new game, but I can't reach them privately — /skip to open it up to anyone!</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

<TelegramChat label="Example: the turn reminder in a private chat">
  <TgMessage from="Nani Pix">⏰ Reminder: it's your turn to start a new game! DM me a screenshot whenever you're ready, or send /newgame to pick one instead.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

If they haven't started within 12 hours, the turn opens to everyone and Nani Pix says
so in the topic: "⌛ Nobody started a new game in time — it's open to anyone now!"

**When Nani Pix misses a right answer.** If someone gave a correct answer under a
name she didn't recognise, the round's creator can award them the win with
`/correct @username` in the game topic. It works only while the round is running,
and only once somebody has guessed: it's for a guess she missed, not a way to
skip to the end. The creator can't award the win to themselves or to Nani Pix, and
she has to know the player: anyone who has ever written to her or chatted
in the topic is fine, otherwise they need to message her first.

### The clue album

Every ending that shows the answer (a win, an unsolved round, `/pass`, and
`/stop` with reveal) is followed by a second post, with no pin: an album
captioned **🖼 Clues from this game**. It holds every extra screenshot that was
bought during the round, in the order they were handed out, in full quality
and unpixelated. Refunded ones are in it too. In
[HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/), the album starts with the other
screenshot of the pair, since the video shows only one of them. More than 10
pictures go out as several albums, a single picture as a plain photo, and if
there's nothing to show, nothing is posted.

## Nobody wins

A round ends **unsolved** when:

- the last try on stage 5 is a wrong guess,
- nobody guesses on stage 5 for 6 hours, or
- **two days** pass since the round went live, however many guesses were made, or
- the group **gives up** with `/pass` (see below).

If nobody guesses for 3 hours, Nani Pix first nudges the topic. After 6 hours without
a guess she posts the next, clearer stage by herself, so a round nobody touches still
ends within 30 hours.

Nani Pix reveals the screenshot and the title with the same kind of video as a win,
just without the gold badge. If the video can't be made in time, you get the plain
screenshot instead. The [clue album](#the-clue-album) follows it. Any [bounty](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/#spending-in-public)
goes back to the players who put it up, and the reveal says so.

An unsolved round doesn't hand the turn to anyone: if the turn was open, it stays
open.

  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

### Giving up with /pass

Nobody has a clue? Send `/pass` in the game topic to vote to give up. Send it again to
take your vote back. The round's host can't vote, since they know the answer.

When enough players agree, the round ends unsolved right away, exactly like the
two-day limit: the reveal, the bounty back to its contributors. "Enough" is half of
the people who guessed or hosted a round in the last 24 hours, and never fewer than 3.
Every reply shows the count, like `2/3`.

Votes count for the current stage only: once a clearer picture comes up, the vote
starts again. In HARD MODE, giving up still opens the usual vote for the closest
guess when someone has guessed.

## Stopping a round

The round's creator, or any group admin, can stop a round by sending `/stop` to
Nani Pix in a **private message**. A round she started has no human creator, so only
an admin can stop it. She always asks first:

- **🛑 Yes, stop it** ends the round quietly.
- **🔎 Stop and reveal** ends it and shows the group what the anime was, with the
  same reveal video as an unsolved round.
- **❌ No, keep going** leaves it running.

"Stop and reveal" is offered only for a round that's already live in the topic. A
round still being set up hasn't shown anything to the group yet, so `/stop` just
asks yes or no and cancels it.

<TelegramChat label="Example: stopping a round from a private chat">
  <TgMessage out>/stop</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="Nani Pix" buttons={[['🛑 Yes, stop it'], ['🔎 Stop and reveal'], ['❌ No, keep going']]}>Stop the current game (Whisper of the Heart)? This can't be undone — the turn will open up for anyone to start a new game.</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="Nani Pix">Game stopped — the answer was posted to the group.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

<TelegramChat label="Example: what the game topic sees after Stop and reveal">
  <TgMessage from="Nani Pix" image={original}>
    🛑 The game was stopped. It was Whisper of the Heart.

    Anyone can start a new one now!

    🎲 Game #142
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

A stopped round doesn't count as a win or a loss. Any clues bought and any bounty
are refunded in full, and the turn opens to everyone. This includes a
[HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/#the-vote) round whose vote is still
open: stopping it cancels the vote.

## Passing your turn

If it's your turn and you don't want to start a round, use `/skip` in the game
topic:

- `/skip` opens the turn to everyone.
- `/skip @username` hands it to someone specific. They get the same reminder and
  12-hour window. As with `/correct`, Nani Pix has to know them, and you can't hand
  the turn to her.

`/skip` only works while no round is running, and only for the player whose turn it
is. When the turn is already open to everyone, there's nothing to skip, and Nani Pix
says so.

<TelegramChat label="Example: passing the turn">
  <TgMessage out>/skip @yuki</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="Nani Pix">Turn passed to @yuki.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

<TelegramChat label="Example: opening the turn to everyone">
  <TgMessage out>/skip</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="Nani Pix">Turn opened up — anyone can start the next game!</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

## When Nani Pix starts a round herself

If the group's admins have turned it on, Nani Pix can start rounds by herself:

- **When nobody starts one for 24 hours** after the turn opened up.
- **Occasionally right after a round ends.** There's a small chance (about 1 in 8)
  that she "steals" the next turn. If you've just won, you still keep the win
  and everything it earned; you just don't get to host the next round. Losing your
  turn this way does come with a consolation: a hidden
  [achievement](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/).

Rounds Nani Pix starts are always [HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/).

    📸 Turn 1/2 — guesses: 1/1

    🏷 HARD MODE clue sale: −20% on every clue this round.

    🎲 Game #143

    🛒 https://t.me/your_bot?start=shop
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

## Quiet hours

Your group may have **quiet hours** set, for example overnight. During them Nani Pix
sends nothing on her own: no reminders, no automatic stage changes, no timeouts, no
rounds of her own. Her timers are paused too, so quiet time doesn't count towards
them. Everything you do still works normally, including guessing and winning.
