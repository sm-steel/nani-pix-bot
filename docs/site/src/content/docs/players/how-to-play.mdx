Nani Pix runs a guessing game in one topic of your Telegram group. One player
picks an anime screenshot, Nani Pix posts it **heavily pixelated**, and everyone
else tries to name the anime. Every wrong guess brings the picture closer to
sharp, so the first stages are where the real bragging rights are.

## A round, step by step

1. **It's your turn** — after you win a round, when someone hands you the turn,
   or whenever the turn is open to everyone.
2. **Start a round in a private chat with Nani Pix.** Send her a screenshot, or send
   `/newgame` and pick a real screenshot from her sources instead. See
   [Starting a round](/nani-pix-bot/players/starting-a-round/).
3. **Tell Nani Pix which anime it is** by searching AniList, Shikimori, Tenrai or
   TMDB, picking from your MyAnimeList list, or typing the title yourself.
4. **Check the private preview** of all five stages and confirm. Nani Pix posts
   stage 1 to the group's game topic.
5. **Everyone guesses** with `/guess <title>` in the game topic. A wrong guess
   uses up one of the stage's tries; when they run out, the next, clearer stage
   is posted. See [Guessing and stages](/nani-pix-bot/players/guessing/).
6. **The first correct guess wins.** Nani Pix reveals the original screenshot, and
   the winner gets the next turn. If nobody gets it after stage 5, the answer is
   revealed and the round ends unsolved.

## Things worth knowing

- **You can't guess on your own round** — you already know the answer.
- **Guesses earn pixels 💠**, the game's currency. Spend them on private clues in
  the shop. See [Pixels and the clue shop](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/).
- **Sometimes Nani Pix starts a round herself.** Those rounds are
  [HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/): two screenshots, two turns, one
  try per turn.
- **Playing earns achievements and titles**, and every week, month and year has
  its champions. See [Achievements and titles](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/).
- Lost? Send `/help` to Nani Pix at any time, or see the full
  [command list](/nani-pix-bot/players/commands/).
