Rounds are set up in a **private chat with the bot**, so nobody in the group sees
the answer. You can start one when it's your turn, or when the turn is open to
everyone, and only while no other round is running. You also have to be a member
of the group.

When you start, the bot tells the group that you're preparing a round, so nobody
else tries at the same moment. You then have **one hour** to finish the setup. If
you don't, the setup is dropped and the turn opens to everyone.

## Two ways in

- **Send a screenshot.** The bot first shows it back to you at stage 1, the
  blockiest stage, so you can judge whether it makes a good puzzle.
- **Send `/newgame`** if you don't have a screenshot to hand. You identify the
  anime first, then pick a real screenshot for it (see below).

## Tell the bot which anime it is

The bot asks how you'd like to identify the anime:

<TelegramChat label="Example: choosing how to identify the anime">
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" buttons={[['AniList', 'Shikimori'], ['Tenrai', 'TMDB'], ['Manual entry'], ['My MAL List']]}>
    How would you like to identify the anime?
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

- **AniList, Shikimori, Tenrai or TMDB**: type the name the way you remember it,
  then tap the right one of up to five results. **None of these — search again**
  lets you retry, and **↩ Different search method** brings you back to this menu
  if the anime isn't on that site. Shikimori usually has the best Russian titles,
  so when the bot speaks Russian it lists Shikimori first. TMDB only knows English
  titles.
- **Manual entry**: for an anime none of the sites know. Type the title, then at
  least one alternative title. Only what you type will count as a correct guess.
- **My MAL List**: shown when the group's bot has MyAnimeList linking turned on.
  You browse your own MyAnimeList list, 10 entries at a time with every status
  included, and pick from it. The first time, the bot walks you through linking:
  open the link, log in to MyAnimeList, approve access, and paste the code you're
  shown back into the chat. You can also link ahead of time with `/linkmal` and
  unlink with `/unlinkmal`.

**More names from other sites.** Once the anime is identified, the bot looks it up
on the other sites as well, and may offer titles it found there as checkboxes. Tick
the ones that should also count as a correct guess, then tap **➕ Add selected**,
or **⏭ Skip**.

**Do the numbers matter?** Guessing normally ignores numbers, so `GITS` wins
*GITS 2026*. When a title is mostly numbers, like *91 Days*, the bot asks you
whether the numbers should count.

## Picking a screenshot (after `/newgame`)

1. Choose a source: Shikimori, Tenrai or TMDB. The site you identified the anime
   on comes first. **📤 Upload my own instead** is always there, and you can simply
   send a photo at any point.
2. Browse the gallery, five numbered screenshots at a time, using **◀️ Back** and
   **More ▶️**.
3. If the screenshots are from a different anime, tap **❌ Wrong anime? Search again**.
4. Tap a number to pick that screenshot.

If a site is down or has nothing, the bot says so and shows the source menu again
with that site marked ⚠️. You can try another site, type a different search, or
upload your own screenshot.

## The preview

Before anything is posted, the bot shows you privately how the round will play
out: the screenshot at all five stages, and every answer that will count. From
there you can:

- **🖼️ Change image**: upload a different screenshot, or pick another one from the
  same gallery.
- **🔍 Re-search title**: identify the anime again.
- **📝 Add a synonym**: add more answers that should count.
- **🔢 Numbers count**: shown when a title has a number in it. Turn it on if the
  numbers have to be part of the guess.
- **🎨 Pixelation**: choose how the blocks are coloured. The default, median,
  suits most screenshots. The preview is redrawn straight away so you can compare.
- **💰 Bounty**: put 30, 60 or 100 💠 into the round's pot for whoever solves it.
- **✅ Confirm and start**: posts stage 1 to the game topic. The round is live.

Changed your mind? Send `/stop` to cancel the setup.
