Seasons reward **cosmetics**: items that change how you and your games look. Owning one
is permanent. Send `/cosmetics` to the bot in a private chat to see what you own, preview
it and equip it.

<TelegramChat label="Example: the /cosmetics menu">
  <TgMessage out>/cosmetics</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">
    🎨 Your cosmetics:
    🖼 Stage frame: 1 owned · equipped: Border
    🌸 Name flair: 1 owned · equipped: nothing
    …
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

Tap a slot to list its items (the equipped one is marked ✅), then tap an item: the bot
sends its preview, followed by **Equip** or **Unequip**. You can equip one item per slot.

## Slots

| Slot | Where it shows | Preview |
|---|---|---|
| 🎉 Win effect | the celebration over your name at the end of the reveal video, when you win with `/guess` or the starter's `/correct` | a short clip |
| 🎬 Reveal transition | the reveal video of the rounds you start | a short clip |
| 🖼 Stage frame | the border on the pixelated images of the rounds you start | an image |
| 🌸 Name flair | an emoji after your name in the group's messages | a line of text |
| 💬 Guess reaction | the reaction the bot puts on your winning `/guess` | a line of text |
| 🪪 Profile banner | the background of your [profile card](/nani-pix-bot/players/profile/) | an image |
| 🏷 Title | shown next to your name — pick it with [`/title`](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#titles) | a line of text |

A few limits:

- A win by a vote or by an admin's `/setwinner` has no reveal video, so it has no win effect.
- Rounds the bot starts by itself (HARD MODE) never use the starter's transition or frame.
- Flair is text only: images such as the profile card and the reveal video show your
  plain name.

Besides the pass, a season may sell cosmetics in the [Season Shop](/nani-pix-bot/players/season-shop/).

Previews are drawn on demand. If the bot can't show one right now, it says so instead —
try again later.
