## Making a guess

Guess with **`/guess <title>`** in the group's game topic. Ordinary messages are
never treated as guesses, so you can chat freely. Anyone in the group can guess,
except the player who set up the round.

The bot is forgiving about how you write the title:

- **Case and punctuation don't matter**: `re zero` matches *Re:Zero*.
- **Numbers and seasons are ignored**: `shingeki no kyojin` wins *Shingeki no
  Kyojin Season 2*, and `GITS` wins *GITS 2026*. The round's creator can switch
  this off for titles where the numbers matter.
- **Any accepted title works**: English, romaji, Japanese, Russian, or any synonym
  the creator added.
- **Small typos are fine**: the bot accepts titles that are close enough.

A wrong guess gets a reply telling you how many tries are left on this stage,
plus any pixels 💠 it just earned you (see
[Pixels and the clue shop](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/)). A winning
guess shows its earnings under the reveal the same way.

<TelegramChat label="Example: a wrong guess">
  <TgMessage out>/guess tokyo story</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">
    ❌ Not quite! Guesses until the next clue: {third.wrongGuessLimit - 1}/{third.wrongGuessLimit} (stage 3/5).

    💠 +2 for Kenji
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

`/guess` only works while a round is being played. With no round running, or
while the group is voting on a [HARD MODE](/nani-pix-bot/players/hard-mode/) round,
the bot says so instead:

<TelegramChat label="Example: guessing when there is nothing to guess">
  <TgMessage out>/guess cowboy bebop</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">No game is currently running. DM me a screenshot to start one, or send /newgame to pick one instead!</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage out>/guess cowboy bebop</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">🗳 Nobody got this one, so it's up to the group: vote on who was actually right in the poll above.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

### The fine print of matching

- **Apostrophes are dropped**, not turned into spaces: `journeys` matches
  *Journey's*.
- **Full-width characters count as ordinary ones**: `ＦＲＩＥＲＥＮ` is the same
  guess as `frieren`.
- **Roman numerals and ordinals are numbers too**: a standalone `I` to `XX`, and
  ordinals like `2nd` or `2-й`, are ignored along with digits and the word
  "season".
- **A guess made only of numbers never wins a title that has words.** `2026` on its
  own doesn't win *GITS 2026*.
- **Titles made only of numbers still work.** For a title like *22/7*, the
  numbers are all there is, so they're kept: `22/7`, `22 7` and `227` all win.

### Partly right

If your wrong guess shares whole words with the answer, the bot shows the title
with just those words spelled out, in the same reply:

<TelegramChat label="Example: a partly right guess">
  <TgMessage out>/guess frieren the mage</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">
    ❌ Not quite! Guesses until the next clue: {third.wrongGuessLimit - 1}/{third.wrongGuessLimit} (stage 3/5).

    🔎 Partly right: _ _ _ _ _ _ &nbsp; _ _ &nbsp; Frieren

    💠 +2 for Mio
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

- Only words of three letters or more count, and together they need at least
  **4 matched letters** by default. Your group's admins can change that number or
  turn partial reveals off (see
  [Game settings](/nani-pix-bot/admins/game-settings/)).
- When the answer has several titles (English, romaji and so on), the bot shows
  the one your guess matched best.
- The bot never reveals the whole title this way. A guess that names every word of
  the title reveals nothing at all.
- It's still a wrong guess and still uses up a try. If that try was the stage's
  last, the reveal appears in the caption of the next stage's picture instead.
- Once anything has been revealed this way, the 📏 Title shape clue is taken out
  of the shop for the rest of the round. Anyone who already bought it keeps it.

## The five stages

Each round starts at stage 1, the blockiest version of the screenshot. Every stage
allows a set number of wrong guesses. Once they're used up, the bot posts the next,
clearer stage. If the last try on stage 5 is wrong, the round ends unsolved.

With the default settings, the wrong guesses allowed per stage are:

<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Stage</th><th>Wrong guesses before it sharpens</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>{stages.map((s) => <tr><td>{s.stage}</td><td>{s.wrongGuessLimit}</td></tr>)}</tbody>

So the round starts unforgiving and loosens as the picture gets clearer. Your
group's admins can change these numbers. The bot's replies always show the
current stage's tries as *left/allowed*.

**The picture also sharpens on its own.** If nobody guesses for 3 hours the bot
sends a reminder, and after 6 hours without a guess it moves to the next stage by
itself. Any guess resets that clock. Players can also pay to sharpen the picture
early with `/sharpen` (see [Pixels and the clue shop](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/)).

## Where the round stands: /status

The current picture is always pinned in the game topic. If the chat ever looks out
of step with the bot (a picture that never arrived, a missing pin), send
**`/status`** in the game topic. During a round, the bot posts the current stage's
picture again with a caption saying where things stand: the stage and the tries
left on it, the bounty if there is one, how long until the round ends on its own,
and the round's number.

<TelegramChat label="Example: /status during a round">
  <TgMessage out>/status</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot" image={stage3}>
    📸 Stage 3/5 · guesses left at this stage: {third.wrongGuessLimit - 1}/{third.wrongGuessLimit}

    💰 Bounty: 80 💠

    ⏳ Ends on its own in 1d 7h if nobody solves it

    🎲 Game #88
  </TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

In a HARD MODE round it shows both screenshots and the turn instead of the stage.
Between rounds there's no picture to show, so `/status` says what's going on: who
is setting up the next round, that a vote is running, whose turn it is, or that
anyone can start one.

<TelegramChat label="Example: /status between rounds">
  <TgMessage out>/status</TgMessage>
  <TgMessage from="nani-pix-bot">🎯 No game running — it's @kenji's turn to start the next one.</TgMessage>
</TelegramChat>

`/status` only shows things. It never changes the round, and the picture it posts
isn't pinned: the pin stays on the round's own stage post.
