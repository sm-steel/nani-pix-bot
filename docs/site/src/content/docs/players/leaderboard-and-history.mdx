## Leaderboard

`/leaderboard` is the all-time table of everyone who has won at least one round, 10
players per page. For each player it shows their title, 👑 wins, 💠 pixel balance
and 🏆 achievement points. Players are ranked by wins, then points.

It works in the game topic at any time, and in a private message with the bot. In a
private message it also shows your own place when you're not on the page shown.

A HARD MODE win counts as 2 wins here.

## Standings

`/standings` shows this week's, month's and year's champion races. See
[Champions](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#champions) for how they're scored.

## History

`/history` (in a private message) lists the group's finished rounds, newest first,
10 per page. Rounds still being set up, played or voted on never appear, so the
history can't spoil anything.

Each row shows the 🎲 game number, the date it ended, the anime, the winner (❌ if
nobody won) and who hosted it. Tabs at the top narrow the list:

- **📜 All**: every finished round
- **👤 I played**: rounds you hosted, won or guessed in
- **🎲 I hosted**: rounds you hosted
- **👑 I won**: rounds you won

Tap a round's number to open its record:

- **📋 Record**: the titles, the host, where the anime and screenshot came from,
  when it started and ended, and how it ended (who won, at which stage, and how; or
  why nobody did), plus the clues bought, the bounty and each stage change.
- **💬 Guesses**: every guess made in the round, with who made it, at which stage,
  and whether it was right.

The 🎲 game number also appears on every post about a round, so you can look one up
or mention it to an admin.
