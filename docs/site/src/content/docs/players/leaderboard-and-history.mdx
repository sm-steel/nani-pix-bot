## Leaderboard

`/leaderboard` is the all-time table of everyone who has won at least one round, 10
players per page. For each player it shows their title (in «» after the name), 👑
wins, 💠 pixel balance and 🏆 achievement points. Players are ranked by wins, then
points; if both are equal, a fixed order decides, so no two players ever share a
place.

<LeaderboardMock label="Example: the leaderboard" header="👑 Leaderboard" player="Player" />

When there's more than one page, the **◀ 1/3 ▶** buttons under it turn the page
in the same message instead of posting a new one.

It works in the game topic at any time, and in a private message with the bot for
members of the group. In a private message it also shows your own place when
you're not on the page shown, e.g. *You: #14 · 3 👑 · 220 💠 · 12 🏆*.

A HARD MODE win counts as 2 wins here.

## Standings

`/standings` shows this week's, month's and year's champion races. See
[Champions](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#champions) for how they're scored
and what the message looks like.

## History

`/history` works in a private message, for members of the group. It lists the
group's finished rounds, newest first, 10 per page. Rounds still being set up,
played or voted on never appear, so the history can't spoil anything. Dates and
times are in the group's time zone: the one admins set for
[quiet hours](/nani-pix-bot/admins/schedule/#quiet-hours), or UTC if none is set.

Each row shows the 🎲 game number, the date it ended, the anime, the winner (❌ if
nobody won) and who hosted it. Under the list there's a button for each round's
number, ◀ ▶ to turn pages, and four tabs that narrow the list (the current one is
marked ●):

- **📜 All**: every finished round
- **👤 I played**: rounds you hosted, won or guessed in
- **🎲 I hosted**: rounds you hosted
- **👑 I won**: rounds you won

Tap a round's number to open its record. It has two tabs:

- **📋 Record**, where it opens:
  - the titles, the host, how the anime was found and where the screenshot came
    from, and whether it was HARD MODE
  - when it started and ended, and how long it lasted
  - how it ended: who won, at which stage (or turn), and how (with `/guess`,
    confirmed by the host with `/correct`, by the group's vote, or named by an
    admin), how long it took to solve, the bounty paid out and the 🌟 standings
    points the winner got. For an unsolved round, why nobody won.
  - 🛒 the clues bought: when, by whom, which clue, what it cost and when the buyer
    shared it (— if they never did). A refunded clue isn't listed.
  - 💰 the bounty: what each player put in
  - 🎞 the stages: when each new stage came and why (the wrong-guess limit was
    reached, someone paid to `/sharpen`, or 6 hours passed with no guess). A HARD
    MODE round has turns instead, so it has no stages section.
  - 👥 how many players guessed
  - 🗳 in a HARD MODE round that went to a vote, every vote: who voted for whom

  Anything a round didn't have is left out.
- **💬 Guesses**, with the count on the tab: every guess made in the round, with
  the time, who made it, the guess, the stage and whether it was right (✅/❌), 25
  per page. Very old rounds from before guesses were recorded show *No guess log
  for this game — guesses weren't recorded yet back then.*

**◀ Back to the list**, on either tab, returns to the page of the list you came
from.
