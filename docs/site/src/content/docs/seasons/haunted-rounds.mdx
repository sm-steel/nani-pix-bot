In [Yōkai Night](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/yokai-night/), a spirit may get into a round.
When a season round goes live, Nani Pix rolls for it: about **1 round in 4** is haunted.
The rest of the season's rounds play as usual.

## What a haunting does

A haunted round gets one of four twists, picked at random. The twist changes the
pixelated pictures you guess from, nothing else.

| Twist | What you see | At the last stage |
|---|---|---|
| **Kitsune illusion** | the picture is mirrored | stays |
| **Tanuki leaf** | the colours are bewitched | clears |
| **Yūrei double** | a ghostly double drifts over the picture | clears |
| **Kyōkotsu X-ray** | the picture is an X-ray negative | clears |

- Stage 1's caption announces the haunting: the twist, what it does, and the 🪙 bonus.
  `/status` says it again, for the picture you're looking at.
- On the last stage the Tanuki leaf, the Yūrei double and the Kyōkotsu X-ray lift, and the
  caption says so. Only the Kitsune mirror stays to the end.
- The *Reveal a tile* clue shows the twisted picture too, so the tile you tap matches
  what you see.
- **The reveal is always clean**: the video and the final picture show the real
  screenshot.

## What a haunting pays

Every Season Coin 🪙 of a haunted round is **doubled**, for everyone in it: the winner,
the host and each player's first-guess bonus. Season XP stays the same.

Haunted wins also count toward:

- the **Bone Collector** objective (win 2 haunted rounds) and the **Ghost Hunter**
  achievement (see [Yōkai Night](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/yokai-night/#achievements));
- the **Bone Collector set**: 7 haunted wins assemble the ☠️ Mini Skeleton flair (see
  [Bones](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/hundred-candles/#bones));
- [100 Candles](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/hundred-candles/): a haunted round snuffs 2 candles
  instead of 1.

## The Night Parade

**31 October** is the Night Parade, the whole day from midnight to midnight in the
group's time zone (the quiet-hours one, or UTC when none is set). On that day:

- **every round is haunted**;
- **the theme is narrower**: only mythology or supernatural anime can be played;
- **🪙 are doubled again**, on top of the haunting, so a Night Parade round pays **×4 🪙**;
- each finished round snuffs **3 candles** for [100 Candles](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/hundred-candles/).

A round counts as a Night Parade round when it goes live that day, even if it ends after
midnight.

Nani Pix posts in the game topic when the parade begins, with the day's rules, and again
at midnight when it's over (her "sunrise" post). Quiet hours hold those posts like any other. Winning on the
Night Parade climbs the **Night Parade** achievement.

The Night Parade only happens when the season runs on 31 October: admins, see
[Scheduling seasons](/nani-pix-bot/admins/seasons/).
