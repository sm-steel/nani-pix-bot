In [Yōkai Night](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/yokai-night/), the whole group plays toward one
goal: put out **100 candles**. Every finished season round snuffs some. When the last one
goes out, everyone who helped is rewarded, and Nani Pix wakes the **Gashadokuro**, a
giant skeleton, for one boss game.

## Snuffing candles

A season round snuffs candles when it ends, won or unsolved:

| Round | Candles |
|---|---|
| A normal round | 1 |
| A [haunted round](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/haunted-rounds/) | 2 |
| A round on the [Night Parade](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/haunted-rounds/#the-night-parade) | 3 |
| The Gashadokuro boss game | 0 |

A round that's still running, or one that was stopped, snuffs nothing.

Nani Pix posts the counter card in the game topic at **25, 50 and 75** candles. The skulls
pile up and the Gashadokuro climbs out of the ground as the count rises.

### /candles

Send `/candles` in the game topic or in a private chat with Nani Pix to see the counter
card: how many candles are out, how much each round snuffs, the next milestone, whether
you've helped yet, your [bones](#bones), and, once the goal is reached, how the boss game
is going.

## Reaching 100

When a round puts out the 100th candle, every player who helped gets **50 🪙** and the
title **Gashadokuro Slayer**. You helped if, in a finished season round before that
moment, you:

- made at least one guess, right or wrong;
- hosted it (HARD MODE rounds don't count: Nani Pix hosts those);
- or won it.

The reward is paid once. Players who join in after the 100th candle don't get it.

## The Gashadokuro

Once the candles are out, Nani Pix starts the **boss game** herself, as soon as no round
is running and quiet hours allow. She takes the next turn for it, even from the last
round's winner. The boss game is a normal 5-stage round with an anime from the season's
theme, but:

- **every stage is haunted**, and the twists pile up: stage 1 is mirrored, stage 2 adds
  the Tanuki leaf, stage 3 the Yūrei double, and stages 4 and 5 add the Kyōkotsu X-ray.
  None of them lift. The reveal is still clean.
- **guessers earn ×2 Season XP and ×3 🪙**, on top of the haunting's ×2 🪙, so ×6 🪙 in all.
  Nani Pix hosts it, so there's no host reward.
- it snuffs no candles.

The boss game happens once per season. If it is stopped, it comes back. It doesn't start
once the season is closing.

## Bones

Every win of a haunted round, or of the boss game, earns you a **bone** 🦴, and Nani Pix
tells you in a private message. Collect **7** and she assembles the **☠️ Mini Skeleton**
name flair for you: the Bone Collector set. Equip it in
[`/cosmetics`](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/cosmetics/).

Bones are only counted while the season runs, and the flair is yours to keep. `/candles`
shows how many bones you have.
