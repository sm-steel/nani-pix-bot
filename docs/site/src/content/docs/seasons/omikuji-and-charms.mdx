[Yōkai Night](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/yokai-night/) has a shrine: draw a fortune slip
every day, and carry two charms, the **Ofuda 📜** and the **Omamori 🧿**.

## Omikuji

Send `/omikuji` in the game topic or in a private chat with Nani Pix to draw a slip.
You get **one a day**; the day turns at midnight in the group's time zone.

| Slip | Chance | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 大吉 **Daikichi**, great blessing | 10% | 20 🪙 and an Omamori 🧿 |
| 吉 **Kichi**, blessing | 30% | 10 🪙 |
| 小吉 **Shōkichi**, small blessing | 40% | 5 🪙 |
| 凶 **Kyō**, a curse | 20% | a free prank [hex](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/hexes/) on yourself |

The slip comes back as a card with your picture.

**Kyō** puts a Nopperabō or a Chōchin-obake on you for 24 hours, picked at random. It
costs nothing, and an Omamori doesn't stop it. If you've opted out of hexes, or already
carry one, the spirits pass you by and nothing happens. A kyō that lands is always
public: drawn in a private chat, its card goes to the game topic too.

Omikuji only open during a season that has them.

## Your charms: /inventory

Send `/inventory` in the game topic or in a private chat to see your clue tokens 🎟,
Ofuda 📜 and Omamori 🧿. Charms don't expire: what you don't use stays with you for the
next season that has them.

| Charm | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| 📜 Ofuda | The [Season Shop](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/season-shop/), 20 🪙, up to 2 per player |
| 🧿 Omamori | The [pass](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/yokai-night/#the-pass), tier 7; a Daikichi slip |

## Ofuda

An Ofuda **forgives one wrong guess**: the miss doesn't count toward the stage's
wrong-guess limit, so it can't move the round on to the next stage or end it.

1. During a round, open [`/shop`](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/) in a private
   chat with Nani Pix and tap **📜 Arm an Ofuda**. Arming is private; the topic sees
   nothing.
2. Your next wrong guess in that round is forgiven. The Ofuda burns away and the topic
   gets a card saying that miss didn't count.

- An armed Ofuda is used up **only when it forgives a miss**. If the round ends first,
  you keep it.
- One armed at a time, and only for the round that's running.
- You can arm one only while a season that has Ofuda is on, like Yōkai Night. Outside
  one, the button isn't shown and your Ofuda wait in `/inventory`. One you armed before
  the season ended still works in that round.
- It doesn't work in HARD MODE rounds, or in your own round, since you can't guess there.
- A forgiven miss is still a guess: it earns what any miss earns, and it still sets off a
  [Kitsune hex](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/hexes/).

## Omamori

An Omamori is never armed: just hold one and it **blocks the next hex** someone casts on
you. See [Blocking a hex](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/hexes/#blocking-a-hex-the-omamori).
