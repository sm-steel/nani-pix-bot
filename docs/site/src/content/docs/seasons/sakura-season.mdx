**🌸 Sakura Season** is the first season Nani Pix runs. Its games are slice-of-life, romance
and school anime under a watercolor wash, clues cost a quarter less, and there is a
pass, a shop, objectives, a stamp for your profile and three achievements of its own.
The group's admins pick when it runs (see [Scheduling seasons](/nani-pix-bot/admins/seasons/)).

![Sakura Season: a cherry-blossom schoolyard with the season's stamp and its gradient title](../../../assets/generated/season-sakura-plain-en.webp)

This page lists everything the season gives. How the pieces work in general is on the
pages for [Season Coins and the Season Pass](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/season-pass/), the
[Season Shop](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/season-shop/), [cosmetics](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/cosmetics/),
your [profile](/nani-pix-bot/players/profile/) and
[achievements](/nani-pix-bot/players/achievements/#season-achievements).

## The rules of the season

- **Theme.** Only **slice-of-life, romance or school anime** can be played in a season
  game. An anime qualifies when it carries the *Slice of Life* or *Romance* genre or the
  *School* theme. When you pick one that doesn't, Nani Pix says right away which kind of
  anime is allowed and asks for another, before you choose a screenshot. That holds
  whether you found the anime by search, picked it from your MAL list or typed its name. When she can't tell what genre the anime is, she asks you to
  pick a different one, or to find it by name in Shikimori or Tenrai search.
- **Watercolor.** The pixelated pictures of the season's games are painted in a
  watercolor style.
- **Clues cost 25% less** in the season's games, on top of any HARD MODE sale (see
  [Season clue discount](/nani-pix-bot/players/pixels-and-shop/#season-clue-discount)).
- **Premium costs 300 💠**, and leftover 🪙 turn into 💠 at 2 for 1 when the season ends.

| You won at stage 5 | 10 | 25 |

| Your round ended unsolved | 5 | 5 |

Coins are the same at every stage; XP is bigger the earlier you win. An admin naming a
winner for your unsolved round tops you up to the solved amounts.

## Stamps

Once the season ends, every player who scored in it gets the **Sakura stamp** on their profile card.
Finish the free pass (tier 12) and the stamp gets a **gold ring**. Claim the ⭐ Premium
reward of tier 10 and the stamp turns into the golden **Sakura+** one.

![A profile card with three Sakura stamps: the plain one, the one with a gold ring and the golden Sakura+](../../../assets/generated/profile-stamps-en.webp)

From left to right: scored in the season; completed the free pass; and Sakura+ (shown
here with the gold ring too, for a player who also finished the free pass). See
[Your profile](/nani-pix-bot/players/profile/) for choosing which stamps your card shows.

## Achievements

Sakura Season has three achievements of its own, each earned once: two are listed
here, and a hidden one.

| Achievement | How | Rarity |
|---|---|---|
| **Kokuhaku** | Win two season games on the same day | 🥈 |
| **Hanami Party** | Play a season game with 4 or more guessers | 🥉 |

Hanami Party counts every player who guessed in the game, not only its winner and its
host. When a game ends, each of them is checked for achievements.
