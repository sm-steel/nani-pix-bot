**🎃 Yōkai Night** is Nani Pix's Halloween season. Its games are horror, supernatural,
mythology and vampire anime under a moonlit sky, and Japan's spirits come out to play:
some rounds are haunted, one night is the Night Parade, the whole group snuffs 100 candles
to wake a giant skeleton, and players can hex each other. Nani Pix dresses up too. The
group's admins pick when it runs (see [Scheduling seasons](/nani-pix-bot/admins/seasons/)).

![Yōkai Night: a moonlit shrine with the season's stamp and its gradient title](../../../assets/generated/season-yokai-plain-en.webp)

This page lists everything the season gives. Its new mechanics each have their own page:

- [Haunted rounds and the Night Parade](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/haunted-rounds/): about
  1 round in 4 is haunted, and pays double 🪙;
- [100 Candles](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/hundred-candles/): a group goal that ends in the
  Gashadokuro boss game;
- [Hexes](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/hexes/): curse a fellow player with 🪙;
- [Omikuji and charms](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/omikuji-and-charms/): a daily fortune slip,
  the Ofuda 📜 and the Omamori 🧿.

The pass, the shop and cosmetics work as in every season: see
[Season Coins and the Season Pass](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/season-pass/), the
[Season Shop](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/season-shop/) and [cosmetics](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/cosmetics/).

## The rules of the season

- **Theme.** Only **horror, supernatural, mythology or vampire anime** can be played in a
  season game. An anime qualifies when it carries the *Horror* or *Supernatural* genre or
  the *Mythology* or *Vampire* theme. Nani Pix checks it the same way as in
  [Sakura Season](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/sakura-season/#the-rules-of-the-season): she says
  right away when an anime doesn't fit, and asks for another. On the
  [Night Parade](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/haunted-rounds/#the-night-parade) the theme is
  narrower.
- **Moonlight.** The pixelated pictures of the season's games get a night look on their
  edges: an indigo tint, moonlight from the top left, the moon with a tiny witch on a broom
  in one top corner and bats with a spider in the other. The centre of the picture stays
  exactly as pixelated.
- **Haunted rounds.** About **25%** of the season's rounds are haunted: the picture is
  twisted, and every 🪙 of the round is doubled. See
  [Haunted rounds](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/haunted-rounds/).
- **Nani Pix in costume.** While the season runs she wears pumpkin accessories, or the
  costume of whoever started the round (see [Nani Pix's costume](/nani-pix-bot/seasons/cosmetics/#nani-pixs-costume)).
- **No clue discount.** Clues cost the usual price in this season.
- **Premium costs 300 💠**, and leftover 🪙 turn into 💠 at 2 for 1 when the season ends.

## Stamps

Once the season ends, every player who scored in it gets the **Yōkai Night stamp** on
their profile card. Finish the free pass (tier 12) and the stamp gets a **gold ring**.
Claim the ⭐ Premium reward of tier 10 and it turns into the **gold Yōkai stamp**.

![A profile card with three Yōkai Night stamps: the plain one, the one with a gold ring and the gold one](../../../assets/generated/profile-yokai-stamps-en.webp)
