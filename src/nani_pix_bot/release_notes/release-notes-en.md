### ✨ New
- Numbers no longer trip up a `/guess`: `GITS` wins "GITS 2026" (and the other way round), and season numbers, "2nd", roman numerals, the word "season" and fancy symbols like × or « » are all ignored.
- The letter clues now only ever hand out real letters, and the title shape shows digits as `X` and the word "season" in plain sight — unless the whole title is a number, like "22/7".
- 🔢 A game's creator can now make the numbers in a title count: the preview has a "Numbers count" switch, and the bot asks outright when a title is mostly numbers, like "91 Days".
- 🔎 When you set up a game, the bot looks the anime up on the other sites too and offers any names it's missing as checkboxes — tick the ones that should also count as a correct guess.
- 📖 There's now an illustrated guide, in English and Russian, covering how to play, every command and how to run your own bot: https://sm-steel.github.io/nani-pix-bot/
- 👮 Admins can schedule and manage seasons with `/season` (seasons themselves arrive in a later update).
- 🎁 Admins can hand out season cosmetics with `/grantitem`.
- 🪪 `/profile [@username]` shows a player's profile card: avatar, title, a stamp for every season played and how many cosmetics they own. ◀ ▶ on the card flip through everyone's cards, and 👥 Players lists them all. Up to four of your rarest achievements show too, and ✏️ on your own card (in a private chat) lets you pick which achievements and season stamps appear.
- 🎨 `/cosmetics` (in a private chat) lists the season cosmetics you own by slot, previews each one — as an image, a short clip or a line of text — and lets you equip it: a win effect, a reveal transition and a stage frame for your rounds, a name flair emoji after your name in the group, a reaction on your winning guess, a profile banner and season titles.
- 🪙 Season games will earn Season Coins for wins, hosting and first guesses, and a Season Pass with free and ⭐ Premium rewards (Premium costs 💠) pays out cosmetics, coins, 💠 and clue tokens as your Season XP grows: see it with `/seasonpass`, and leftover 🪙 turn into 💠 when the season ends (seasons themselves arrive in a later update).
- 🎟 A clue token makes your next clue in the shop free, and you get it back if the clue can't be delivered.
- 🛍 A season may open a Season Shop: `/seasonshop` (in a private chat) lets you look at an item first, then buy it with 🪙 — cosmetics once each, clue tokens 🎟 up to a limit — and you put cosmetics on in `/cosmetics` (seasons themselves arrive in a later update).
- 🎯 A season may set objectives: one-time goals that pay Season XP and 🪙, with a private message when you finish one and your progress, and the date you finished, in `/seasonpass`.
- 🌸 A season may make clues cheaper in its games, stacking with the HARD MODE sale, and may paint its games' stage pictures in its own style.
